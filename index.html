The Changing Landscape

Why Producing Good Evidence Is No Longer Enough

Global health has entered a rapidly shifting era where three concurrent challenges make standard academic publishing inadequate on its own.

Force 01

AI & The Deluge of Information

Artificial intelligence is reshaping how scientific conclusions are generated, summarized, and consumed. When automated algorithms flatten statistical nuance into punchy soundbites, critical caveats disappear unless researchers lead the story.

Force 02

Misinformation Speed vs. Rigor

Health misinformation moves through digital networks at alarming speeds with emotive production value. Research locked inside 25-page PDF journals cannot compete against misinformation without compelling visual translation.

Force 03

Complex Funding & Policy Climate

Global health programs operate in an increasingly volatile funding landscape. Decision-makers and citizens need to understand what health funding actually achieves in human terms, beyond raw budget lines.

The Academic Isolation Trap

"A health intervention should not be represented only as a budget line or an impenetrable statistic."

When researchers simply publish and walk away, the vacuum is filled by confusion, rumor, or indifference. Science Beyond the Paper bridges this void with human-centered, scientifically verified storytelling.

The Advocacy Dimension

"More Than a Budget Line"

When global health programs encounter changing funding priorities and fiscal pressures, people need to understand what investments in global health actually accomplish.

A healthcare intervention should not be represented only as a budget item or an abstract statistic. We must illuminate the full reality: the evidence, the people behind the numbers, the outcomes achieved, the remaining limitations, and the human cost of alternative choices.

What We Reveal to The World:

The dedicated frontline workers, families, and communities behind the numbers.
The concrete medical outcomes and lives preserved through scientific discoveries.
The real constraints and unanswered questions that require continued honest inquiry.
The consequences of policy decisions and funding shifts on community health.

Better communication fosters an informed public conversation about global health while maintaining unwavering scientific integrity.

KEYNOTE DECLARATION • ALPHONCE OYUGI

Science Beyond the Paper: The Five-Minute Message

Global health is entering a rapidly changing era. Artificial intelligence is transforming how information is produced and consumed, misinformation can spread rapidly, and global health programs are operating in an increasingly complex funding and policy environment.

In this context, producing good evidence is no longer enough. We also need to become much better at communicating why that evidence matters.

My idea is to build Science Beyond the Paper as an interdisciplinary science communication and public engagement initiative that brings researchers together with filmmakers, journalists, designers, educators, technology professionals, policymakers, and communities.

Together, we will transform important global health research into documentaries, visual stories, digital content, and educational resources that make evidence accessible without compromising scientific rigor.

This is more than a communication project. It is a leadership and capacity-building movement. When global health programs face changing priorities and funding pressures, people need to understand what investments in global health actually accomplish. A health intervention should not be represented only as a budget line or a statistic.

"We should not leave science inside the paper. We should build the skills, partnerships, and platforms needed to take it into communities, conversations, and sustainable actions."
